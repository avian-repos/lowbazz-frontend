Common questions

Do shops need any special equipment?

No. A smartphone and an internet connection are enough to receive orders and manage stock.

How does Lowbazz make money?

A small commission on each order and optional paid tools for shops, such as promotions and analytics.

Which cities will you launch in?

We plan to begin with a few dense neighbourhoods and expand wherever local stores sign up.

What can customers order?

Anything a participating local shop stocks, such as groceries, dairy, stationery and pharmacy items, subject to local regulations.
